# Hack for Miami

Our project for the **Hack for Miami** hackathon.

## Theme: Four Forces, One City

Miami is shaped by four elemental forces. Our project looks at how each one affects the city and the people who live here:

| Force | Focus |
|-------|-------|
| 💧 **Water** | Sea-level rise, flooding, storm surge, water quality |
| 🌬️ **Air** | Air quality, heat, hurricanes and wind |
| 🌱 **Land** | Urban development, green space, soil and ecosystems |
| 🔥 **Fire** | Extreme heat, energy, wildfire and emergency response |
